Quick Reference

Actions

Take any Action at any time if you can pay its Credit cost.

Recipes

Purchase the Top Recipe
Cost labeled on Recipe
  • Pay immediately; place the Recipe or put it in Storage.
  • Storage holds up to 2 Recipes.
  • Only Purchased Recipes in Storage or on the Game Board may be viewed.
  • You will not need every Recipe to complete a Level.
  • Providing a multiple of the required inputs for a Recipe will produce outputs proportionally.

Building

Move a placed Recipe
1 Credit
Place Recipes from Storage
Free
Swap 2 placed Recipes
2 Credits
Rotate a placed Recipe
Free
  • Place Recipes on any Tile, in any orientation.
  • Adjacent Recipes transfer Resources directly. Recipes that are not adjacent need Conveyor connections.
  • A Recipe from Storage may replace a placed Recipe for free.
  • Inputs stay on marked Input Tiles and cannot rotate. They may swap marked Input Tiles at any time.
  • Objective Outputs stay on marked Output Tiles, may rotate, and may swap marked Output Tiles at any time.
  • Place Optional Outputs on any Tile during Setup. They may be moved and grant their labeled Credits once.

Conveyors

Place Straight Conveyors
Free
Alter or move a Straight Conveyor
Free
Place a Curved or Split Conveyor
1 Credit
Rotate or move an existing Curved or Split Conveyor
Free
Remove a Curved or Split Conveyor
Refund 1 Credit
  • Keep Conveyors on yellow Tile borders; no diagonals.
  • They may cross as bridges but never share the same yellow border.
  • No bridges over Recipes, Inputs, Outputs, or empty Tiles.
  • A Conveyor may transport more than one type of Resource.
  • You choose whether an adjacent Conveyor connects to an Input, Recipe or Output.
  • There are no merging Conveyors. Curves or Splits MAY be used to create a merger.

Switch On

  • All Recipes activate together.
  • All Resources move together.
  • Win when every Objective Output is produced and connected to its Tile.
  • Producing more than an Objective requires still completes it.
  • Completing a Level with 0 Credits is still a win. Reaching 0 before completion loses the Level.
  • Leftover Credits determine that Level’s EVS and do not carry over.
